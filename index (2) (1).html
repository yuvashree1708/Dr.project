Common questions

Before You Visit.

Clear answers to typical questions asked by our new and returning patients.

How can I book an appointment?
Fill in the Consultation Request Form above with your name, email, phone number, and preferred date and time, or call or WhatsApp the clinic. Your request stays pending until the clinic confirms it.
Where is Confi Dental Smilez located?
The clinic is at No. 29, Saroja Ammal Complex, First Floor, Medavakkam Main Road, Keelkattalai, Chennai 600117. It is above Saroja Ammal Complex, near the bus terminus.
What dental treatments are available?
The clinic covers dental implants, crowns and bridges, root canal treatment, dentures and tooth replacement, restorative dentistry, and cosmetic dentistry. Whether a treatment suits you is decided during a consultation with the dentist.
How do I know which treatment is right for me?
A consultation with a professional dentist is the only way to assess your needs. The information on this website is educational and does not replace a diagnosis.
Can I enquire about dental implants?
Yes. Use the form above or call or WhatsApp the clinic. Whether implants are appropriate for you is assessed at consultation.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Bring any previous dental records, X-rays, and a list of your current medications if you have them. The clinic will confirm any other details when it contacts you.
How can I ask about treatment costs?
Call or WhatsApp the clinic, or ask during your consultation. Costs depend on the treatment and are not listed on this website.
Does submitting an appointment request confirm my booking?
No. A request is an enquiry. Your appointment is only confirmed after the clinic has contacted you and confirmed the slot.
